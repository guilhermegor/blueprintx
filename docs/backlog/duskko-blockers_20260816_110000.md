replacing the `contexto_duskko/config/consultas_sql/` queries), processing, persistence in
**SQLite**. Environments: **Windows + XP's TLS proxy** *and* Linux/WSL. A **second developer**
(Werner) is coming on, in a **bilingual** project (pt-BR docs, English code).

These five facts are the cutoff criterion for everything that follows.

(#109). That is why order matters.
